Welcome to YOUR new CS 1.5 Server!
========================================
Classic Counter-Strike. Good games. Have fun!

CHAT: SOUNDS & STATS
/soundlist      All sound commands
/sounds         Alternative sound list
/soundson       Enable sounds
/soundsoff      Mute sounds
/rank           Your ranking
/top15          Best players
/statsme        Your statistics
/hp             Last opponent's remaining HP

CHAT: MAPS
maps or /maps   Open the map nomination menu
<map name>      Nominate, e.g. de_rats_2001
nextmap         Next map
timeleft        Time remaining
rtv or /rtv     Request a map vote

CONSOLE
amxmodmenu      Admin menu (admins only)
